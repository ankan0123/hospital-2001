THE VISIONARY SCIENTIST

A Life Dedicated to Science & Humanity

Late Prof. Dr. B. N. Chakravarty was not merely a physician; he was a pioneer whose relentless curiosity broke boundaries in human reproduction. During the early 1980s, when in vitro fertilisation was in its infancy globally, Prof. Chakravarty understood that assisted reproduction had to be brought to India with uncompromising scientific ethics and affordability.

Under his stewardship, IRM Kolkata grew from a pioneering clinical research unit into a world-renowned centre of excellence in reproductive endocrinology, advanced micromanipulation, and embryology. He insisted on patient dignity, zero commercialization, and deep research into the underlying causes of recurrent implantation failure and male subfertility.

“Infertility is not just an inability to conceive; it is an unvoiced sorrow that burdens the soul. Our duty as doctors is not merely to manipulate gametes, but to restore peace, hope, and wholeness to the family.”

— Late Prof. Dr. B. N. Chakravarty
CHRONICLE OF EXCELLENCE

Milestones in Reproductive Medicine

A historical overview of foundational breakthroughs achieved under Prof. B. N. Chakravarty's guidance.

1986

Establishment of the Institute of Reproductive Medicine

Prof. Chakravarty founded IRM in Entally, Kolkata, as India's premier autonomous institute for reproductive clinical research and patient care.

1990

Pioneering Donor Oocyte IVF in Eastern India

Achieved one of India's earliest successful pregnancies using donor oocyte protocols for women with premature ovarian insufficiency.

1994

Introduction of ICSI & Micromanipulation

Setup Eastern India's first Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection workstation, providing hope for couples afflicted with severe male azoospermia.

2004

Launch of Post-Doctoral Fellowship in Reproductive Medicine

Recognized by the National Board of Examinations (NBE) and state universities to train future generations of IVF specialists across South Asia.
